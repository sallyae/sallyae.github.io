Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 16, 2026

Overview

Sally AI Assistant ("Sally," "we," "us") is a personal AI assistant service operated for private, non-commercial use. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our SMS messaging service.

Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from Sally, we collect:

  • Phone number: Your mobile phone number, provided directly by you
  • Message content: The content of messages you send to and receive from our service
  • Opt-in records: Records of your consent to receive messages

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Send you SMS notifications about sports court bookings, scheduling updates, and reminders
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Maintain records of your messaging preferences

Information Sharing and Mobile Data Protection

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information — including your mobile phone number and any data collected as part of the SMS opt-in process — with any third parties for their own marketing, advertising, or promotional purposes. Your phone number and message content are kept strictly confidential.

We may use third-party service providers (such as Twilio) solely to deliver messages on our behalf, and they are contractually prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than message delivery. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

We retain your phone number and opt-in records for as long as you are subscribed to our messaging service. Message logs may be retained for up to 90 days for service quality purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time
  • Get help: Reply HELP to receive assistance information
  • Request deletion: Contact us to request deletion of your personal data

SMS Messaging Terms

  • Message frequency varies based on your booking activity
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages
  • Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
